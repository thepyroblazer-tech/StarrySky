✦ StarrySky

StarrySky controls Spotify the same way your lock screen does. It works with free Spotify, and you don't need to log in.

It needs one permission so it can see what's playing:

  1. Tap Open notification access, find StarrySky and switch it on.
  2. If Android says “Restricted setting”, tap Open app info, then the ⋮ menu at the top right → Allow restricted settings, and try step 1 again. Android does this for apps installed outside the Play Store.
  3. Come back here. StarrySky will start by itself.
